- Ground every idea in the ads and the research brief. Don't invent stats, testimonials or customer quotes. Quotes come only from the research brief, word for word.
- Competitor claims are never safe to copy just because they're running. If a winning ad's strength is a claim we can't make, borrow the structure and swap in a promise from the research brief.
- Write hooks the way people talk. Short. Specific. No hype words.
