# Step 2: Inside the buyer's head

We're writing direct response copy for {{audience}} (ages {{age_range}}) in {{niche}} who struggle with {{problem}}. Your job is to figure out how these people think, feel, and talk.

## What you're working with

- Customer quotes from {{communities}}, app store reviews, comments, and forums: {{voc_json}}
- Competitor ads and transcripts: {{ads_json}}

Answer every question below in plain language, then back it up with real quotes from the data, each with a source link. Their words matter more than your summary.

## Part 1: Who they are

- Describe this person in a short paragraph, like you know them.
- What's their general outlook? Social, money, and cultural attitudes that shape how they see things (only what the data actually shows).
- What do they want most out of life?
- Where have they won? Where have they failed or fallen short?
- What do *they* blame for not having the life they want? (Other people, the system, bad luck, timing, themselves.)
- What are they biased against or quick to dismiss?
- Their core beliefs about life, relationships, and family, in one to three sentences.

## Part 2: What they've already tried

- List what this market uses today to deal with the problem.
- How has that gone for them?
- What do they like about those options?
- What do they hate about them?
- Any bad experiences or horror stories?
- Do they believe the current options actually work? If not, why not?

## Part 3: The unusual fix

- Has anyone ever tried to solve this problem in a strange or unexpected way? What happened?
- Is there a story people tell about why the usual fixes don't work, like someone with an interest in keeping things the way they are?
- Was there an older approach (decades back or more) that worked, or seemed to, and then got forgotten? Why did it fade?

Only include things you can back with a source. A real, surprising story is gold. A made-up one gets us banned and kills trust.

## Part 4: What made it worse

- Do people believe this problem used to not exist, or used to be milder?
- Do they think something recent made it worse? (New tech, habits, culture, work changes, anything specific to this niche.)
- What are those forces, and why do people think they exist?
- Is there a group of people who don't seem to have this problem? What's different about how they live?

## Rules

- Every quote must come from the data with a source link. Never write a quote yourself.
- If the data doesn't answer a question, write "not enough data" instead of guessing.
- For health products, don't turn beliefs into medical claims. Note them as beliefs.
