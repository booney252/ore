## The rules for a creator version

- **Keep word for word**: every line that explains the problem, why it happens (the mechanism, timing, protein, anything technical), the product payoff (features and what they fix) and the CTA. These were approved; don't improve them.
- **Change only what makes it theirs**: the line(s) that say who they are, the personal details in the story (age, kids, job, gym, where they are when it happens), small word swaps so it sounds like how they talk, and anything that would be unbelievable for them. A version can be a little shorter or longer, but keep the same beats in the same order.
- **Write a new text hook** for them: 3-8 words, opens a second loop, never repeats their spoken hook. The loop it opens has to be answered by something the script actually says ("#2 is why your cravings come back" works because #2 explains it); never promise a moment, person or reveal the script doesn't have.
- No overlay, shot or cut directions: those are up to the creator.
- Follow the brand's hard rules in "What Ore knows" (e.g. names that can't be said) and the creator's Avoid list.
