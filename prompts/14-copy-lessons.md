## What to write

Rewrite BOTH lists in full, with the new feedback folded in:
- Turn each point of feedback into a rule Ore can follow while writing: specific, testable, and phrased as what to do ("Every list item gets its own 'why it matters' line..."), not as a summary of what was said. Include the reason in a few words when it helps.
- Keep what the feedback praised as a rule too ("Keep: ..."), so good choices don't get lost.
- If the feedback asks an open question or needs a decision (e.g. "direct creators to vary the hook, or let them?"), write the rule as your best recommendation and mark it **(decide)** so a person can confirm it.
- Merge with the existing lessons: don't repeat a rule that's already there; sharpen it instead. If the new feedback contradicts an old lesson, the new feedback wins; drop or rewrite the old one.
- Facts about the product that came with the feedback (e.g. why a feature matters) belong in brand lessons, worded as what copy should say, and only as the person stated them. Don't add facts they didn't give. For a health product, keep every rule inside what ads can claim: no promised results, no dosing advice; if the feedback implies a claim that needs compliance review, add "(compliance check)".
- After each rule, cite where it came from in brackets, e.g. [{{source_tag}}].
- Keep each list tight: the most important rules first, at most about 25 per list.
