# Step 4: The master research brief

We're running direct response marketing for {{product}} in {{niche}}, aimed at {{audience}} (ages {{age_range}}) in {{country}}.

Below is everything from the earlier research steps: competitors, buyer psychology, and market awareness. Read all of it closely, then write **one master brief**. This brief becomes the background doc for every ad, script, and piece of copy we make from here on, by me and by other AI tools. Make it complete enough that someone who has never seen the research could write good ads from it.

Leave out market size and leave out strategy recommendations from the earlier steps. I only want the sections below.

## Inputs

- Competitor teardown: {{step1_output}}
- Buyer psychology: {{step2_output}}
- Market awareness: {{step3_output}}
- Customer quotes: {{voc_json}}

## Sections

### 1. Who we're talking to
Focus on people who are **problem aware** or **just starting to look at solutions**. Describe them: age, life situation, daily reality.

### 2. What's going on inside them

**Pains.** A bullet list of their specific problems and how those problems make them feel, written the way they'd describe it. Be concrete: the moments, the embarrassment, the frustration, what they avoid doing because of it. Back the strongest ones with real quotes.

**The dream.** What life looks like once this is solved. How they'd look, feel, and act. What else is true about their life in that picture. Make it vivid and specific.

**How they see themselves.** Their identity, and the words to use and the words to avoid when talking to them. Include phrases they actually use.

**Promises we can make.** A bullet list of the outcomes they most want to hear, in the order of how much they'd care. Each promise must be something the product can honestly deliver.

### 3. Objections
List the biggest reasons they'd scroll past, doubt us, or not buy. Use the objections customers actually raise in the data. For each one, give a short way to answer it in an ad.

### 4. What they've already tried
The solutions this market has already used, and specifically why each one wasn't good enough for them.

## Rules

- Quotes only from the data, each with its source link.
- Write it so it's easy to scan: short bullets, plain language.
- For health or results products: no cure claims, no specific numbers or timelines unless the brand has proof, no before and after promises. Flag any promise that needs compliance review.
