# Step 3: How aware is this market?

Figure out how aware {{audience}} (ages {{age_range}}) in {{country}} are of the problem {{product}} solves and of products like it.

## The five stages

- **Unaware**: they don't know they have the problem, or don't see it as a problem.
- **Problem aware**: they feel the problem but don't know solutions exist.
- **Solution aware**: they know solutions exist but haven't picked one.
- **Product aware**: they know specific products in this category and are comparing them.
- **Most aware**: they know the category well and are ready to buy.

For this exercise, "product aware" and "most aware" mean awareness of products *like* ours ({{category_examples}}), not our brand specifically.

## What you're working with

- Customer quotes: {{voc_json}}
- Competitors and their ads: {{competitors_json}}, {{ads_json}}
- Web search for: search interest, app store category data, creator and influencer content in this space, articles, and any category growth or user numbers. Source link for every fact.

Look for clues in how people talk. Someone describing symptoms without naming a fix is problem aware. Someone asking "which app is best" is product aware.
