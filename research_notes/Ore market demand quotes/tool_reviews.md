## Q1. What gaps do reviewers say existing tools leave (research vs. briefs/scripts vs. iteration)?

### Takeaway
Research/analytics tools (Foreplay, Motion) are praised for organizing and reporting, but repeatedly described as stopping before the work: Foreplay "stops at the brief stage", Motion "reports performance rather than acting on it", and Motion insights are "high-level" and depend on strict naming conventions. AI generators (Arcads, Creatify, Atria) attempt the output step but reviewers say they ignore scripts and brand guidelines and look fake. Nobody in the reviews found joins research, winner diagnosis, script writing and creator handoff in one loop. That gap is inferred from the reviews; no reviewer states it in those words.

### Inferences
- Positioning in third-party comparisons: Foreplay = research and brief; Motion = analytics on own ads; Atria = research and AI drafts. The repeated complaints are about price relative to research-only value (Foreplay), insights that need a human to turn into action (Motion), and AI output nobody can use (Atria). That leaves room for a tool whose output is a script a creator can film, built from winners' diagnosed elements.
- Motion's per-creator grouping depends on naming conventions, so per-creator results tracking is effectively manual setup today.

Brand-side: inconsistent creator quality even with detailed briefs, briefs not followed, markups/expiring credits. ## Q3. Which features get requested repeatedly (incl. AI script tools)?

### Takeaway
For AI UGC/script tools, the repeated asks are: follow my script and brand guidelines, natural/realistic output, don't burn credits on every fix, transparent billing and cancellation, and human support. Across marketplaces: non-expiring credits, fair dispute handling, a bigger creator pool. For analytics: cross-channel coverage, fatigue alerts, insights deep enough to act on.

### Inferences
- Script fidelity ("unrelated to the script I supplied", "ignored brand guidelines") is the top product complaint about AI UGC tools. Real creators filming a strong script avoids the realism problem entirely; Ore's "script for a real creator" position doesn't compete on AI avatars.
- Billing complaints (surprise renewals, expiring credits, hard cancellation) show up for nearly every tool (Arcads, Creatify, Atria, Insense, Billo). Transparent, non-expiring pricing and free revisions (Ore's pricing note says "revisions are free") is a cheap point of difference.
- Per-creator results tracking was not requested in any review found. The demand there is unproven, not disproven.

### Gaps
- No reviews found for Minea/Kalodata; not searched further given the tool budget and weaker fit with Meta ad briefs.
- No verbatim reviews of Jasper/ChatGPT used for UGC scripts were collected (mostly on Reddit/X, which weren't reachable).
- Trustpilot samples skew negative for SaaS (unhappy users self-select), and some positive reviews are "Invited". G2 (more positive, mostly invited) could not be read directly.
