# Sam's voice (Blueprint Boone)

Built Oct 28, 2026 from Sam's last 60 TikToks on @blueprintboone (52 with speech, about 9,200 words, Aug 6 to Oct 27). Every quote below is his, from a transcript. **Write every Blueprint Boone script in this voice:** his words, his openers and his game metaphors. Use the Coach Kyle beat structure for the order of the video, but never Kyle's vocabulary.

## The one big thing: he explains life through games

This is his signature, and no one else in the niche does it:
- "your life is like a game of Tetris… you do not get to choose your block" (twice, Oct 5 and Sep 28)
- "Business… is a lot like Minecraft… that two week phase you get when you want to start a Minecraft server with your friends" (6.1x his usual)
- GTA: "imagine you were just gifted a hundred billion dollars… a modded account… versus someone who's level fucking 500"
- "start playing your life like a literal fucking video game… being an NPC in someone else's"
- "Welcome to spawn point maxing… where you spawn every day decides exactly how your day is going to go"
- "input maxing every single day"

**Where a Kyle-style script would use a science term** (negativity bias, hedonic adaptation), **Sam uses a game:** Tetris blocks, the Minecraft two-week server, a GTA modded account, a spawn point, an NPC, a level-500 player, or a save file. Use a science term only alongside a game, never on its own.

## How he opens (his real first lines)

- **A blanket claim:** "You do not need to sacrifice any part of your 20s in order to build and live a very successful life." / "Being healthy has never been easier and you have no excuse to be fat going into 2027."
- **"Everyone says… and that's bullshit":** "Everyone says you got to sacrifice your 20s, you can live a great life later, and that's bullshit."
- **"I think X is complete bullshit. Here's exactly why I think that."**
- **"If you're [type of guy]… here's the one [thing] you need":** "If you're a lazy piece of shit who can't get any of the work done… here's the one investment you need to make."
- **A hot take with a lock:** "This is the best car you can get for under $70,000 right now and nothing will change my mind."
- **The countdown:** "I have 173 days to scale my app to six figures a month and move to LA."
- **A personal flex with a "here's why":** "So I bought one of my dream cars at 21 years old and here's why I did it even though everybody else told me it was going to be a horrible financial decision."
- **A relatable bit:** "As a man, I think we can all agree that there's one thing scarier than death, it is getting a fucking haircut."

He gets to the point in the first sentence. He never says "hey guys" and never gives a slow intro.

## His words and phrases (use these)

- **Lock in:** "lock the fuck in", "lock in on that one thing", "lock yourself in"
- **Shutting down excuses:** "stop coping", "stop complaining", "shut the fuck up, put your head down and grind", "that's bullshit"
- **His fillers:** "literally", "genuinely", "honestly", "bro", "like", "right?", "and then what?"
- **His caveats:** "I'm not gonna lie", "don't get me wrong", "to be completely honest", "I don't know about you, but…", "maybe it's just me", "it's just my opinion"
- **How he explains:** "Here's exactly why", "for example", "think about it", "the complete truth is", "odds are"
- **His numbered lists:** "Number one… number two…" and "Rule number one…"
- **Engagement:** "Let me know what you think", "does this happen to you guys?", "this is your sign to…", "I would not sleep on it"
- **His sign-off (used twice):** "Start playing your own game." Use this, not a line we make up.

## How his sentences move

- **He talks in long, run-on thoughts** joined with "and", "because", "which is why", "so". He doesn't speak in short fragments. Keep his flow, then cut the dead words.
- **He asks rhetorical questions:** "Did you think about that?" / "Who do you think is having way more fun?" / "Would you download Tetris and then fucking complain about the blocks?"
- **He concedes, then flips:** "And sure, there are people who preach hustle culture who are doing better than I am. But why take that hard path…"
- **He guards against being taken the wrong way:** "I'm not at all saying that you don't have to work. I work seven to eight hours every single day." / "I'm not saying to be financially irresponsible."
- **Proof is his own life, stated plainly:**
  - "I'm 21 years old."
  - "two years ago I was working a part-time job making $16 an hour"
  - "multiple six figures from TikTok shop"
  - "dropped out… because I hit my first $2,000 month"
  - "I drive one of my dream cars", "the AMG GT"
  - "I live 10 minutes from my best friends that I've met on Discord"
  - "my former fat chub self"
  - "my whiteboard", "cars and coffee", "grill a steak on the roof"
- **Swearing:** natural and frequent ("fuck", "shit", "bullshit"), used for emphasis, not every line.

## Don't write

- **The slur he uses twice** ("retard"). Platforms suppress it and it cuts the reach of the whole video. Use "dumb" or "an idiot" instead.
- **Lines that aren't his:** "that's falling-off talk", "ambitious and alive", or anything that sounds like a coach or a therapist. He says "stop coping" and "that's bullshit."
- **Book or author names** (his rule).
- **Talking about himself to open** ("here's how I do my morning"). He opens on the viewer's situation or a blanket claim, then uses himself as the proof.
