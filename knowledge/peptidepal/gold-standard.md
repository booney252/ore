## Why these work

- **Explanation with a picture.** Half-life is the coffee at 3pm that still keeps you up at 11, then "you're not starting from zero, you're stacking it on top of what's still in there." One listen and you could explain it to a friend.
- **Only one lesson per script, and not every script teaches.** Brief 1 teaches half-life, Brief 2 is pure frustration with the spreadsheet, Brief 3 teaches how peak days eat your muscle.
- **The problem gets dug into before the fix.** Brief 3 goes from "look at your arms" to the birthday photo where she looks deflated, to "it was muscle," to "it comes back as fat." The viewer feels it before PeptidePal shows up.
- **Real, specific moments.** Fixing formulas at 11 at night, a vial that runs out Thursday, the same ten protein foods, a gold star from the calorie app for starving.
- **Sounds like a person.** Long thoughts joined with "and", "so" and "'cause", small asides, a little humor ("I basically gave myself a second job", "a sad cup of cottage cheese").
- **The product shows up late and fixes exactly what was raised,** with real features (the Today screen's still-on-board estimate, peak-day alerts, the calculator, the vial countdown, reminders, protein on the same screen).
