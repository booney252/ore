# PeptidePal rules

Updated 2026-10-21 by the owner. This is the whole list: it replaces the hard rules in the research brief (section 5 and anywhere else the brief lists "rules", "avoid" or "never").

1. Never say actual compound or drug names, in scripts, on-screen text or captions (e.g. BPC-157, GHK-Cu, tesamorelin, sermorelin, TB-500, semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide, and brand names like Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound). This holds even when a creator's own story uses one: "the copper peptide I use for my skin" or "a peptide for belly fat" instead. Everything else is fine: "peptides", "a second peptide", "GLP-1", "your weekly shot", "the pen", doses, half-life, the fridge, the routine.
2. Link every ad to the correct App Store listing (several apps share the name).
3. Don't call it "medication" (or "the medication"), in scripts, text hooks or captions. Say "peptides", "my peptides" or "my GLP-1".
