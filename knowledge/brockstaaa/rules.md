# @brockstaaa: what sells (from real orders, Nov 2026)

These rules come from the owner's own sales, not views. Follow them in every post list.

1. **Every post shows proof the product works.** Slide 2 is a before/after, week 1 vs week 4, one coat vs two, or a close-up result. The posts that sold (milk serum "high maintenance routine", rosemary oil "dead hair", Lip Shot "thin lips") all showed the result.
2. **Slide 1 promises a fix:** "how to unf*ck your…", "nobody talks about this but…", or a flip ("my insanely high maintenance routine…" → "jk it's one serum").
3. **Views aren't sales.** Text threads where someone reacts (mom, bestie) get views but sell less (the mom text: 7.3K views, 1 order). Only use a text thread after the promise and the proof.
4. **Weight the lineup by orders per 1K views**, not views: milk serum 1.9, rosemary oil about 0.7, Lip Shot about 0.25 (13 orders off the 53K post, so it's a volume play).
5. Products that can't show a before/after (supplements, intimate care) need another kind of proof: a text thread or a fake review that stands in for the result.
