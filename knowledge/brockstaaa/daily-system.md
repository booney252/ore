# brockstaaa daily slides: 40 a day, 5 buckets of 8 (decided 2026-11-05)

Every post in the daily plan is tagged with its bucket, so the TikTok page can score each bucket by
orders per 1K views and views per post, and the mix can shift toward what sells.

| Bucket | Posts | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **copy** (copy what's winning) | 8 | A breakout from the tracked accounts' last 7 days, copied directly: the same hook text word for word, the same product, the same format and slide order. Only the pictures change (our own photos, same kind of shot). |
| **plug** (iterate on what's winning) | 8 | A breakout's hook, format and slide order, with one of our lineup products plugged in instead of theirs. |
| **tweak** (iterate on my winners) | 8 | Our own winners (sold, or 3K+ views) with small changes: a tweaked line of copy or a different slide picture. Same structure. |
| **rerun** | 8 | Our winners redone as they were (same hook, same product), new photos only so it isn't a duplicate. |
| **test** (new tests) | 8 | Patterns from what's winning, broken down into our own trend ideas and hook or text formats. Not a copy of any one post. |

Rules for every bucket:
- Slides follow `slide-style.md` (TikTok Sans medium 42px, #FBEEA9, centered, no shadow) and the proof rule in `rules.md` (slide 2 proves it).
- Never repost another account's actual slide images. Copies are rebuilt with our photos.
- Before/afters match (same person, or same hair color and type), and slide 1 matches them.
- No photo twice within 7 days, except product shots.
- A copied or plugged product needs product photos and must be in the TikTok Shop showcase. If there are no photos yet, the plan lists it under "needs product photos" instead of making the post.
