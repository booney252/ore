# Microdosing GLP-1s: what people struggle with and didn't know

Research for PeptidePal, Oct 28, 2026. Sources:
- **Instagram comments:** 226 comments on 7 microdosing reels. Two reels' comments looked like bots (scrambled usernames, generic praise), so I left them out.
- **Articles:** a few news pieces and blogs that quote people who microdose.

Every quote below is word for word, typos included, with its link.

## The big picture

- **About 1 in 7 people on GLP-1 shots have tried microdosing.** In an Evidation survey of 8,486 current injectable users (May 2026), 15% had microdosed. Their reasons: side effects (40.8%), cost (31.9%) and staying at their goal weight (29.6%). ([source](https://www.hackensackmeridianhealth.org/en/healthier-you/2026/05/12/is-microdosing-glp-1s-safe), [source](https://meetlea.ai/blog/glp1-microdosing-does-it-actually-work-evidence-2026))
- **Many microdose without their doctor.** A separate survey of 8,000+ users reportedly found nearly half did it without asking their doctor. That figure is secondhand, so don't put it in an ad.
- **There's no standard "micro" dose, and no trials back any regimen.** Each person is working out their own dose and schedule.
- **Most of it runs on compounded vials.** That means drawing up the dose with a syringe instead of clicking a pen. The FDA has hundreds of reports of people drawing **5 to 20 times** the intended dose from compounded vials. ([source](https://ro.co/weight-loss/microdosing-glp-1s-semaglutide-tirzepatide-ozempic/))

### Two different crowds

1. **Weight people.** Their goals: staying at goal weight, fewer side effects, a lower bill.
2. **Inflammation and health people.** Hashimoto's, RA, endometriosis, PCOS, joint pain, labs, longevity. Many aren't overweight at all. This crowd was surprisingly big in the comments. They care about pain and lab numbers, not the scale.

## The problems, in their words

### 1. The math

> "Is 16units a micro dose? The math confuses me 😂" ([@ape.logan](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbdkFLvgoZe/))

> "How many units on the syringe is 1 mg ? Of Tirzepatide?" ([@4kranchgirl](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbdkFLvgoZe/))

> "What is considered to be a micro dose? Do they not titrate the medication as needed every four weeks?" ([@hollycwebb](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DRSYWJeiJb7/))

Even the experts got confused in the comments. Two people corrected a host's "2.5mg semaglutide" ([@kita.deazevedo](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbdkFLvgoZe/), [@everhealthinstitute](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbdkFLvgoZe/)).

**What they don't know:** the same dose in mg is a different number of units depending on the vial. For example, 1 mg is 10 units from a 10 mg/mL vial but 5 units from a 20 mg/mL vial ([source](https://www.seekpeptides.com/blog/articles/tirzepatide-conversion-chart)). Small doses also land on the tiny marks of the syringe, where misreading by a unit is easy.

### 2. The schedule is made up

> "So then do you take it everyday on micro dose or same injection just less?" ([@saravictoriala](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbdkFLvgoZe/))

> "I take 1.25mg of mounjaro every 5-6 days" ([@ellas_life_90](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbdkFLvgoZe/))

> "I do 1mg every other week." ([@dr.man.nurse07](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dbejma4RKuX/))

> "we are trying to figure out the perfect dose and schedule" ([@saraecalhoun](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dbejma4RKuX/))

**The point:** schedules like every 5 to 6 days, every other week, or a different dose before your period don't fit a "Monday shot" routine. They're easy to lose track of.

### 3. Food noise comes back

> "Wanted to mictodose went to 1,5 the food noise came back hard Scared if I stop completely I will gain plus more back cause the food noise is awful 😔" ([@annouschka_betist](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdB3HQZte_A/))

> "I'm jumping around doses…" and she needed to raise the dose before her period to keep food noise down. (Reddit user Calm_Soft_7865, r/midihealth, quoted secondhand by [Ivy Rx](https://www.ivyrx.com/blog/microdosing-glp-1-reviews))

A clinician explains it: on a lower dose "when you take your next shot, it's as if you're starting over." Symptoms go up and down through the week instead of settling. ([Hackensack Meridian](https://www.hackensackmeridianhealth.org/en/healthier-you/2026/05/12/is-microdosing-glp-1s-safe))

> "Microdosing does nothing for me." ([@wildforwesties](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbdkFLvgoZe/))

> "I'm loosing 1-2 a week sometimes don't lose any is taut normal? , I'm been on it for 7 weeks already how long should o stay on it ?" ([@stellacalabrese](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbdkFLvgoZe/))

> "However what labs do you feel should be tracked ?" ([@mimicnolan](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dbejma4RKuX/), on tirzepatide for RA)

> "I started out super low … It wasn't great for me, I didn't feel much" (Reddit, r/GLP1microdosing, via [Ivy Rx](https://www.ivyrx.com/blog/microdosing-glp-1-reviews))

**The point:** with no set dose, the only way to know what works is to compare your doses with how you felt. Almost nobody is writing that down.

- **Hair loss.** One commenter asked about women on HRT who started tirzepatide "losing so much of their hair when before taking tears appetite, they didn't lose any hair." ([@barb.steinberg](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DRSYWJeiJb7/)) This ties to the hair brief.
- **Mood.** "I couldn't control the tears that would pour out of me" (Jenny Mollen, on a microdose of tirzepatide). "I am quicker to anger than ever" (Jo Piazza, microdosing semaglutide). Both from [Jo Piazza's Substack](https://jopiazza.substack.com/p/the-skinny-on-weight-loss-drug-microdosing).
- **Constipation.** "Little constipation, but outside of that, nothing much else." (Reddit, via [Ivy Rx](https://www.ivyrx.com/blog/microdosing-glp-1-reviews))

## About Brief 2 ("Your Microdose Isn't Micro")

Nobody in this research described doses stacking up past a full dose. I made that story up, so it shouldn't be the gotcha unless a creator actually lived it.

The real, common gotcha is **food noise coming back late in the week, while people blame themselves**. It's backed by real quotes, it's a "wait, that's me" moment, and the on-board estimate pays it off directly.

The **math one** ("the math confuses me 😂") is a strong second.
