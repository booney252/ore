# PeptidePal Iterations: 5 New Ads from the Winners

All five are UGC talking-head videos shot on a phone in a real home, 25-40 seconds long. Each one uses a different segment, pain and opening line from the eight iterations already made.

### Iteration 1: The Three Envelopes
- **From:** ad #6 (PeptidePal own ad, 1114026504421349). Grade B (rising): new, running 18 days and still active, while most PeptidePal ads stop by day 8.
- **Funnel:** TOFU
- **Type:** Pattern transfer.
  - We keep the loving-confrontation scene, the countable detail, the pattern reveal and the comic callback.
  - We swap the fiber, peak-curve and bathroom material for a problem PeptidePal actually solves: unit math.
- **What we're borrowing:**
  - **The opening.** A loved one catches her mid-problem before we know what's wrong. Here, the daughter says "Mom. Why are there three envelopes on the table?" in place of "You've been in there 40 minutes."
  - **The pattern reveal,** almost word for word: "Every week?" / "Every week."
  - **The friend-as-guide device.** The app arrives as someone's tip, not a pitch.
  - **The comic callback and warm close** ("You tried to have tacos on your peak day" / "Keep it."). This becomes the envelopes she tries to save.
- **Why it should work for us:**
  - **Segment:** the Midlife Woman at the buying moment. Her weekly pen is simple, and the second peptide arrives as a vial.
  - **Pain:** "I don't trust my own math." "Vial users mix powder with water and work out syringe units themselves. Pen users don't have this problem."
  - **Stage:** Problem Aware. She doesn't know an app would help, so she hears it from her daughter.
  - **Different from earlier ads:** the reminder gap already went to "I'm Asking Because I Love You." This one is about the math and the number in the log.
  - **Stake:** half-life runs off the amount actually taken. That's why a wrong envelope means a wrong log.
- **Setup:**
  - **Creator:** a woman, 48-58, in a sweater or cardigan, warm.
  - **Location:** her kitchen table at night, under a lamp.
  - **Props:** three envelopes covered in handwritten math sit on the table.
  - **Camera:** propped at chest height.
  - **Performance:** she retells the scene and drops her voice slightly for her daughter's lines.
  - **Length:** about 37 seconds.
- **Alternate hooks:**
  1. "I did the same math three times Sunday night and got three different answers."
     - *First frame:* three envelopes fanned toward the lens, each with a different circled number.
  2. "My daughter caught me doing math on an envelope at nine o'clock at night."
     - *First frame:* the creator looks up from the table, pen in hand, caught.

### Iteration 2: You Have to Get This App

- **Funnel:** BOFU
- **Type:** 90/10.
  - We keep the direct callout hook and the buyer-age face with real screens from frame 0.
  - We swap their breathless feature list for a breathless list of *his own schedules*, then land on two features.
- **What we're borrowing:**
  - **The hook,** nearly word for word: "If you're taking peptides, you have to get this app" becomes "If you run more than one peptide, you have to get this app." Brand rule 1 allows "peptide."
