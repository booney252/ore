# PeptidePal: 2 scripts for Jon

50s, a dad, on peptides (written as a couple of peptides for recovery and sleep). Calm and matter-of-fact, straight to camera in his car. He's the older guy almost no peptide ad talks to.

---

### Iteration 1: I owe my peptides an apology

- **Hook:** "I owe my peptides an apology. I almost quit them over something that was completely my fault."

- **Why it should work for us:** Zafira's confession hook ("We need to publicly apologize... because we lied") broke into Zafira's top 10 at #3. A calm 50-something guy confessing reads as real, not salesy, and his cause (doses sliding, a vial he'd mixed weeks ago) shows off the vial and dose-log features no competitor ad mentions.
