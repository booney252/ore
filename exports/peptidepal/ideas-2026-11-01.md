# PeptidePal: new ideas from the Nov 1 report

From this week's pull: 182 new competitor ads, 79 new breakouts into competitors' top 10s, 70 winners broken down. Full report: `exports/peptidepal/report-2026-11-01.md` (also in the app).

## What changed since Oct 25

- **GLP-1 side effects are the hot lane.** Zafira, Jevawell and Sculptique are spending on exhaustion, constipation and muscle loss on a GLP-1, each selling a pill. Zafira's two new videos broke into its top 10 on Nov 1.
- **Warnings and confessions are breaking in:** Zafira's "We need to publicly apologize... because we lied", the talking bottle's "If you're thinking about ordering... from Amazon, don't", and "This is fake, this is fake...".
- **Our own native ads are rising.** The fiber skit ("You've been in there 40 minutes"), the meme grid ("DAY 5 OF PROTOCOL POOPS GO CRAZY") and both protein cheat sheets are at 28 to 29 days, against a usual life of 7 to 10.
- **We have no live talking-head winner.** Caitlin's car selfie stopped Oct 26 after 59 days, and so did the "$300 vial" meme.
- **Pep AI is moving into GLP-1 progress** with four more rising ads. It still leads with features and compound names on screen.

## 6 new ideas

Already written and not repeated here: the hair script, the food noise script, the prescription one, and the Thanksgiving and doctor's-office ones.

### 1. The Thursday wipeout (talking head, our top priority)
**Pattern:** the side effects competitors sell pills for, plus the peak day only we can show.
**Hook:** "If you're on a GLP-1 and every Thursday you're completely wiped out, it's not random."
**Story:** the creator's bad day lands a day or two after the shot, every week. She thought it was her. Then she saw it on her dose curve and started eating protein and drinking water the day before.
**Why now:** it fills the empty talking-head slot, and it answers what Zafira and Jevawell are spending on, with timing instead of a pill.

### 2. "I need to admit something" (confession)
**Pattern:** warning and confession hooks (#1, #4, #16).
**Hook:** "I need to admit something. For six months I was guessing my doses and calling it a protocol."
**Story:** the mistake every user makes without knowing it: a dose logged from memory, a vial that was older than she thought. Then the log that ended the guessing.

### 3. My first 90 days (time ladder, 75 to 90 seconds)
**Pattern:** the day-by-day ladder (#2, #3, #31), now also in Hone's male-led breakout.
**Beats:**
- Week 1: the shot day hits hard.
- Week 3: she learns her peak days.
- Week 8: she adds a second compound and it gets messy.
- Day 90: it's all on one screen.

**Why:** none of our ads runs over 64 seconds, uses a time ladder or quotes a customer. This does all three.

### 4. Two vials, one fridge (open lane)
**Gap:** the second-compound moment and "did I take it Sunday or Monday?" Still in none of the 32 winners.
**Hook:** "Adding a second peptide is when it stops being simple."
**Open on:** the fridge door with two vials, two schedules and the morning she couldn't remember which one she'd taken.

### 5. The talking vial (animated skit)
**Pattern:** products that talk (Sculptique's tub, Jevawell's prune juice, Zafira's bottle), plus our own cartoon skit, which is rising.
**Gap:** running out mid-cycle, which no ad touches.
**Bit:** a vial in the fridge complaining that nobody knows how much is left in it: "She thinks I've got two weeks. I've got four days." It ends on the vial countdown.

### 6. Same peak day, for the gym guy (men under 40)
**Gap:** men and people under 40. Hone is the only male-led breakout, and 43% of our signups are men, mostly 25 to 44.
**Hook:** "Your lifts aren't getting worse because you're getting older. You're training on your peak day."
**Story:** a lifter who can't eat on peak days, and how he plans protein and training around them.
