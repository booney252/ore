# PeptidePal: why we're making these 3 ads

From the Oct 28 top-10 tracking run (each advertiser's 10 most-seen active ads) and the Oct 28 microdosing research.

## 1. The Hair in Your Hand

**Funnel stage:** TOFU. Problem aware: people on a GLP-1 who are scared by their hair loss and don't know about us yet.

**Why we're doing it:**
- **Our own ad already proved the topic.** PeptidePal's hair loss ad ("…noticing more and more hair filling your brush every single morning…") launched Oct 25 and took 2 of our top 10 spots (#8 and #10) within 3 days. It's a long text-style ad, and this turns it into UGC.
- **Hair loss is a long-runner across competitors:**
  - **Zafira:** "If your hair is coming out in the shower every day…" is its #1 ad, live 153 days.
  - **IM8:** "nobody warned you about the hair in the shower…" is its #1 ad, live 31 days.
  - **KilgourMD:** "If you're on a weight loss medication and your hair is thinning, I promise this is the one thing no one is telling you" is its #9, live 97 days.
- **The pattern:** the shower, the hand or the brush full of hair, plus "nobody warned you." Their payoff is always a supplement. Ours is the thing actually causing it: low protein on peak days, which the app tracks.

## 2. Your Food Noise Has a Schedule

**Funnel stage:** TOFU. Problem aware: people microdosing who think the food noise coming back is their fault.

**Why we're doing it:**
- **Microdosing is a fresh, rising topic.** Noom's #1 ad, live 15 days, is "Microdose GLP-1s starting at $79." Pep AI's #3, live 23 days, speaks to people "estimating your micro-dosages."
- **The audience is big and on their own.** About 1 in 7 people on GLP-1 shots have tried microdosing. There's no standard dose or schedule, so they're working it out themselves.
- **Food noise coming back is a real complaint.** One commenter: "went to 1,5 the food noise came back hard… the food noise is awful 😔". A clinician describes it as "as if you're starting over" with each shot.
- **Nobody is selling to people already doing it.** Competitors sell the microdose itself. Nobody speaks to people who are already microdosing and blaming themselves. We'd own that turn ("it wasn't my willpower, my dose was running low"), and the Today screen's still-in-you estimate pays it off directly.
- **Our own "felt nothing" ad points the same way.** "I'd been running a protocol for weeks and genuinely felt nothing" sits at #3 in our top 10. Not knowing whether your dose is working is a hook that's already working for us.

## 3. What Your Prescription Left Out

**Funnel stage:** MOFU. Solution aware: new starters who know they need more guidance than their doctor gave them and are looking for it.

**Why we're doing it:**
- **Dr. Gordon is all in on this theme.** His #1 ad, live 37 days, opens with "Nobody told me what to expect when I started this medication…". He just launched 2 more versions of it (#7 and #8, Oct 27), and 4 of his other top 10 ads, all under a week old, explain the "nobody told you" side of the shot:
  - **Nausea** (#2, #3): "Nausea is the number one reason people quit…"
  - **The plateau** (#4, #5): "a predictable part of the process that almost nobody explains"
- **Our own ad backs it up.** "Nobody told me how important it was to be eating protein on my protocol" is at #4 in our top 10.
- **Pep AI is on the same idea.** Its #3 is "I thought starting a GLP-1 would be hard until I found this app!"
- **The pattern:** the doctor gave you a pen and a date, and everything that happens in between is on you. Dr. Gordon sells a 30-minute read. We show it on your own schedule: "my 'random' Wednesdays weren't random."
