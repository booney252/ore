# PeptidePal: 3 UGC scripts (v2, after Michael's Oct 29 feedback)

**For every creator:** say it in your own words, with your own personality. Keep the order of the beats and these must-keeps:
- the facts in the protein and levels explanations
- no medication names (say "GLP-1," "my shot")
- no dosing or timing advice
- "PeptidePal, this icon" at the end

**Hook split test:** half the creators open with hook 1 (the open-loop curiosity hook), half with hook 2 (the command hook).

## 2. When Your Food Noise Comes Back

**Funnel stage:** TOFU. Problem aware: people microdosing who aren't perfect with their schedule and think the food noise coming back is their fault.

**Why we're doing it:**
- **Microdosing is a fresh, rising topic.** Noom's #1 ad, live 15 days, is "Microdose GLP-1s starting at $79." Pep AI's #3, live 23 days, speaks to people "estimating your micro-dosages."
- **The audience is big and their schedules are messy.** About 1 in 7 people on GLP-1 shots have tried microdosing, and there's no standard schedule. In the comments, people take it every 5-6 days, every other week, or change it before their period. Michael's research also found only 27-41% of people stay highly consistent with medication in general.
- **Food noise coming back is a real complaint.** One commenter: "went to 1,5 the food noise came back hard… the food noise is awful 😔". A clinician describes each shot as "as if you're starting over."
- **The problem only the app can solve:** on a microdose there's less in you to start with, so a day late, a day early or a skipped shot makes your levels tank on days you can't predict. If you took it the same day every week you wouldn't need us. Almost nobody does.
- **Our own "felt nothing" ad points the same way.** "I'd been running a protocol for weeks and genuinely felt nothing" sits at #3 in our top 10.

3. "Crazy, I just learned why my microdose stopped working some weeks, and it's not what I thought." (open loop, softer)

## 3. What Your Prescription Left Out

**Funnel stage:** MOFU. Solution aware: new starters who know they need more guidance than their doctor gave them and are looking for it.

**Why we're doing it:**
- **Dr. Gordon is all in on this theme.** His #1 ad, live 37 days, opens with "Nobody told me what to expect when I started this medication…". He just launched 2 more versions of it (#7 and #8, Oct 27), and 4 of his other top 10 ads, all under a week old, explain the "nobody told you" side of the shot:
  - **Nausea** (#2, #3): "Nausea is the number one reason people quit…"
  - **The plateau** (#4, #5): "a predictable part of the process that almost nobody explains"
- **Our own ad backs it up.** "Nobody told me how important it was to be eating protein on my protocol" is at #4 in our top 10.
- **Pep AI is on the same idea.** Its #3 is "I thought starting a GLP-1 would be hard until I found this app!"
- **The pattern:** the doctor gave you a pen and a date, and everything that happens in between is on you. Dr. Gordon sells a 30-minute read. We show it on your own schedule, including the weeks you're off it, which is where the app earns its place.
- **To test (Michael's question):** GLP-1 users usually do have a doctor or a telehealth provider, unlike research peptide users. Hook 3 tests the telehealth version, where "they sent it and that was it" is closer to what most people actually get.

**Hooks**

1. "Your doctor handed you your GLP-1, told you once a week, and that was it." (open loop, main)
2. "If you just started a GLP-1, stop googling everything at midnight and listen to this." (command, test arm)
3. "Your telehealth app mailed you a GLP-1, a PDF and a 'message us with questions,' and that was it." (open loop, telehealth version)
