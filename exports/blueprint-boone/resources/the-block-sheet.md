# The Block Sheet

This is the exact thing I use when something goes wrong. Takes 2 minutes. Use it the next time life hands you a block you didn't ask for.

---

## The rule

**When a block drops, you don't get to call it good or bad for 24 hours.**

Think about Tetris. You don't pick your block. The game hands it to you, and the block isn't good or bad, it's just a block. The only thing that matters is where you put it. So stop complaining about the block and start figuring out where it goes.

---

## When a block drops, answer these 4

**1. What actually happened?**
Facts only. No "always", no "never", no "this is the worst". Just what happened.

> 

**2. What story am I telling myself about it?**
Be honest. "This always happens to me." "I'm just not built for this." Write the cope down so you can see it.

> 

**3. What's the other story?**
How would someone who's winning read this exact same block? There's always another story.

> 

**4. Where does this block go?**
One move you can make in the next 24 hours. Not a plan for the year. One move.

> 

---

## My example

**What happened:** Apple took weeks to approve my app and wouldn't answer a single email, so I couldn't launch when I wanted to.

**The story I was telling myself:** "I'm getting held back. Everyone else gets to launch and I'm stuck."

**The other story:** I get extra weeks to dial in the app and build a waitlist before it drops, so launch day is bigger.

**Where the block went:** I started posting more and pushing the waitlist every single day instead of waiting around.
