# The Respawn Sheet

This is what I use after an L, once it's over and I'm still replaying it in my head. Takes 5 minutes. Use it tonight on the one you keep thinking about.

---

## The rule

**Keep the XP. Drop the death screen.**

Every L hands you two things: a lesson and a weight. In any game, when you die, you respawn and you keep all the XP you just earned. You don't sit on the death screen replaying it for three days. You learned where the boss hits, and you go back in.

Most guys do the opposite. They throw away the lesson and carry the weight around for years. Stop coping and respawn.

---

## Death screen check

Be honest. Answer yes or no for the L you're thinking about.

- [ ] I think about it more than once a day.
- [ ] I'm putting off starting the next thing because of it.
- [ ] I tell the story the exact same way every time.
- [ ] I say "I wasted…" or "I should've…" when I talk about it.

**Any yes means you're still on the death screen.** Fill out the 3 lines below.

---

## Respawn in 3 lines

**1. The L**
What happened. One line. Facts only.

> 

**2. The XP**
What it taught you. One sentence. If it takes more than one sentence, you haven't found it yet.

> 

**3. The respawn**
What you're doing differently on the next run. Something you can actually do this week.

> 

Now keep line 2 and drop the rest. If you wrote it on paper, rip up everything except the XP sentence. Literally.
