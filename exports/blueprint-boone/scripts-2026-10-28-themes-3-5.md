**His tools, used inside the beats**
- **Tough love with permission:** "This is harsh and I love you." He can be blunt without being mean.
- **A filter to keep the right viewer:** "If you wanna lock in… like every other influencer on this app, please keep scrolling. So the legends that are still here…" The viewers who stay feel chosen.
- **A retention line mid-video:** "If that statement untangled your brain… and you are still watching, here is how."
- **Self-aware pacing:** "I'll speedrun the rest of these because I'm probably losing your dopamine."
- **A share prompt:** "Save this, send it to the group chat."
- **Coined words:** "certified big boy", "the Big Boy 5", "fit in for framework". Named ideas are what people remember and repeat.

**His pace:** about 150-170 words in 60 seconds. Every line either sets up the next one or pays off the last one. He never says "so basically" or "let me explain," and never recaps.
