## 2. Coach Kyle, taken apart line by line

All the lines below are from his videos: Part 3 ("Big Boy Identity"), Part 2 ("Automating Your Environment"), "Big Boy Maxxing" part 1, "Realistic Weapon-Maxxing", the 330x "10/10 habits" and "Realistic Health-Maxxing". **Every one of his parts does one job, in the same order, in about 60 seconds.** Nothing in them is filler.

| **8. The action** | 54-60s | "Do a full audit on your day-to-day life right now." / "I put together a 10 minute exercise… just comment big boy and I will send it." | Something to do today. The keyword is said right after the value, which is when keywords work. |
| **9. Sign-off** | last 1-2s | "Be better, not perfect." | The same line on every video, a brand stamp the audience repeats back. |

**His tools, used inside the beats**
- **Tough love with permission:** "This is harsh and I love you." He can be blunt without being mean.
- **A filter to keep the right viewer:** "If you wanna lock in… like every other influencer on this app, please keep scrolling. So the legends that are still here…" The viewers who stay feel chosen.
- **A retention line mid-video:** "If that statement untangled your brain… and you are still watching, here is how."
- **Self-aware pacing:** "I'll speedrun the rest of these because I'm probably losing your dopamine."
- **A share prompt:** "Save this, send it to the group chat."
- **Coined words:** "certified big boy", "the Big Boy 5", "fit in for framework". Named ideas are what people remember and repeat.

**His pace:** about 150-170 words in 60 seconds. Every line either sets up the next one or pays off the last one. He never says "so basically" or "let me explain," and never recaps.

## 3. Your version of the formula

Kyle's beats stay the same. Here's what changes:
- **Series names:** "Story-Maxxing" (theme 3), "Fight-Maxxing" (theme 4) and "Clock-Maxxing" (theme 5), each with numbered parts.
- **The in-group line:** Kyle's "little boy talk" becomes **"that's falling-off talk."** It's your audience's own words (lock in, fall off), and it hits the same way.
- **Proof:** Kyle uses his clients. You use your own week building Solis. Those lines are in [brackets]: fill them with real moments.
- **No book or author names**, per your rule. Concepts with a real name (negativity bias, hedonic adaptation) are fine; they do the job of Kyle's "James Clear says."
- **The action:** a comment question for now. Once you have a doc to send, switch it to a keyword, Kyle-style.
- **Sign-off:** "Ambitious and alive." It's your positioning line from the research. Swap it if you have a better one, but use the same one every time.
- **Title box:** "[Series]: [part name] (not for [the wrong guy])", over a celebrity or movie-still panel that changes each beat. See the overlay notes from last time.

**Length:** 55-65s, about 150-170 words. If a line doesn't set up the next one or pay off the last one, cut it.
