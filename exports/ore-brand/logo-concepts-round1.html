Why the spark o

  • It still reads at 34px. The stone and the branch turn to mush that small.
  • It's an o, so it's Ore, and the spark says "we found something" without a word.
  • It sits on the same glow as the site and the app, so the feed, the site and the product look like one thing.
  • Runner-up: the slatted O. It's the most ownable, but it needs a bit more size to read.

All six

The spark o

A lowercase o with a spark: the find in the data, the idea. Simple at 34px, still friendly.

The ore stone

A cut gem on the glow. Ore is raw rock; Ore turns your market into the good stuff.

The branch

One winner splits into three creators. It's the product in one picture, like the tree on the site.

The slatted O

An O built from the site's vertical light slats, filled with the glow. The most ownable look.

The sunrise O

Dark navy with a glowing ring. Moodier, stands out in a white Instagram feed.

The wordmark

Just 'ore' in the site's font on the glow. Cleanest, but the name has to do all the work.
