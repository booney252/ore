## The patterns

1. **The hook is something owners have seen with their own eyes,** named in plain words: yellow foam, legs shaking after a walk, the jump out of the car, crusty eyes. Viewers think "mine does that" in the first second.
2. **"It's not what you think."** "It isn't random and it isn't a stomach bug." "He's not getting old, he's in pain." The second line flips the owner's belief. That's the reason to keep watching.
3. **Props make the body visible.** A hip-bone model, a flask of yellow "bile", a stomach-lining model, ingredient jars. An AI performer can show what a real creator can't easily film.
4. **Labs, specifically.** Every reel says "your Labrador", with breed facts like the Newfoundland fishermen and the full-signal gene. The viewer feels it was made for their dog.
5. **The pitch is "you could do it yourself, but you won't."** He names 3 to 5 ingredients that fix the problem. Then: "sourcing all of that separately is expensive and complicated, and most people quit." Then: "so I use one scoop of Iron Paws." The tip earns trust, and the product is the shortcut.
6. **Two CTAs, two jobs.** REMEDY (a free guide) on the tips and RECIPE on the pitches. Both say "make sure you're following me, otherwise I can't message you", which turns comments into follows and DMs.
7. **Good feelings win the reach.** The top 2 short reels are massage and pleasure (373K, 122K). The top long ones are fear plus hope.

## What's not working, and what's missing

- **He's recycling scripts heavily.** The "brothers / same litter / both 15" skit has run 8 times. "The shake after a walk" has run 5 times, "antlers and hard chews crack teeth" 4 times, and "shedding: two things in your kitchen" 3 times. Most repeats get a fraction of the first post: the brothers skit went 139K, then mostly 10K to 15K. (One exception: the third "shake" post hit 135K.)
- **The newest pitch is flopping.** The "crusty eyes + paw licking + breath + dull coat, you don't need three products" reels from Oct 23 to 28 got 5K to 13K views, under his 18K usual. Bundling four symptoms takes away the one clear thing owners have seen that makes the winners work.
- **Not used yet:**
  - The holidays. Thanksgiving is in 4 weeks, and owners will be asking what their lab can eat.
  - Simple at-home "checks", like the massage (only touched on once, "3 things I check every week").
  - Emotional senior-dog stories, which his audience clearly feels.
  - The lean-lab lifespan study (a real, famous Labrador study).
  - Light humor or a second character beyond the street skit.
