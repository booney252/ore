# @brockstaaa: 5 slideshows for today

Oct 26. Two posts for your winners and three for the test product (Laneige Midnight Minis).

Every one uses patterns the report proved:
- someone else reacts
- a price anchor
- the doubt said out loud and killed
- the "future self" before/after, which won big for the Leefar posts
- the product held back until the last slide, on the vanity, with "you're welcome" or "thank me later"

**Before/after rules from the winners:**
- The "before" photo is ordinary and happy, never gross or shaming. Your own numbers say shame openers flop.
- The "after" is as aspirational as you can get: a party, flash, a night out.
- Pick a before and an after of the **same type of girl** (same hair color and length, similar style) so the swipe reads as one person's glow-up.

**Pinterest tip:** search the terms in *italics* and add "aesthetic" or "pinterest girl". Pick photos with no face filters and no watermarks.
