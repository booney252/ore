## Credits: what costs what

- **ScrapeCreators charges 1 credit per request, not per post.** One request for a TikTok profile returns 10 posts with their views, so that's the cheapest way to check views (10 posts for 1 credit). Looking up one post on its own costs 1 credit.
- **Free:** downloading the slide images and videos, transcribing and breaking them down.
- **Kalodata:** its own points, separate from ScrapeCreators.
- **This pull cost 110 credits.** 5,516 are left.
