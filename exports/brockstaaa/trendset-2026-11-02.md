# @brockstaaa: how to set the trend, Nov 2

From a fresh pull of the last 7 days across the 32 TikTok Shop accounts Ore tracks (1,129 posts, 113 credits). I read the slides of the top 30.

1. **The boyfriend-text gummies post is now at 1.75M**, and everyone is copying it. @shopturbopiggy has four versions (1.75M, 974K, 940K, 75K), with covers that now include "Keeping Him Hooked 101", heels on a bed and a fair at night. It's peaking, so being 10th to post it won't win.
2. **The 2-slide faceless outfit post.** @holistic.wellness.glow is a new account with five posts over 100K this week, the biggest at 1.2M (343x its usual). Every post follows the same pattern:
   - **No face.** The shots are of legs, a mirror selfie with the phone covering the face, or a flat lay.
   - **Same rooms:** a cozy bedroom or a Christmas-lit hallway.
   - **Tiny serif text:** "obsessed with these." then "for the leopard print girls (Sorry about the price)".
   - The pictures look AI-made or heavily styled, so they're cheap to make and always pretty.
3. **Funny holiday sweatshirts (@iluvdealz).** Seven posts over 80K. The biggest are "thankful for you f*ckers" over a vintage family Thanksgiving photo (588K), and the parade sweater posts at 502K and 154K. The format is always 2 slides: a joke or a doubt quote, then the shirt with "(sorry about that price)".
4. **A gift aimed at the boyfriend (@ttsshopfinds, coloring blanket, 64K).** Slide 1 is a Snapchat-style chat: "my man just got me a coloring blanket and I'm obsessed" / "omg i want one so bad but my bf said it's too expensive". Slide 2 is an AI-made cozy scene with "I got you 🙏 / ur girl will love this". It's the only post this week written to the person buying the gift, not the person getting it.
5. **The "6 to a 9" checklist** keeps getting new covers. The latest uses a celebrity with Goli gummies. It still works, but it's getting old.

## What's crowded (skip or twist)

- "How to unf*ck your…" + the "THIRTY??" text thread. Every gummies and lip account posts it now.
- "(sorry about the price)" on every second slide. It still converts, but it's no longer a hook.
- "From a 6 to a 9" checklists.

## Where nobody is yet

Three gaps stand out:
- **Gift buyers who aren't her:** boyfriends, moms, dads.
- **Thanksgiving and Christmas as a moment, not just a backdrop.** Family, the group chat, the office party.
- **Black Friday**, which starts in 3 weeks. Nobody's built a format for it yet.

The winning mechanics are proven: a fake chat, someone reacting, 2 slides, the product last. The move is to take those mechanics to an audience or moment nobody's posting for.

## 8 ideas to set the trend

### 1. "Texts from her group chat" (for the boyfriend buying the gift)
The coloring blanket proved guys can be the audience. Nobody's doing it on purpose yet.
- **Slide 1:** a fake group chat named "girlies 🎀" with screenshots leaking what she wants: "if Jake doesn't get me the Laneige lip set I'm breaking up with him" / "LMAO same" / "send him this".
- **Slide 2:** the product wrapped, with "bro. you've been warned 🎁".
- **Slide 3:** the product with "(it's under $25, you're welcome)".
- **Why it can trend:** it's shareable both ways. Girls send it to their boyfriends ("send him this" is in the post itself). That's built-in sharing, which the gummies post doesn't have.
- **Products:** Laneige or Rhode-style lip sets, Medicube sets, the e.l.f. pickle balm as a stocking stuffer.

### 2. "Your aunt at Thanksgiving" (the family-reaction text thread)
This is the gummies mechanic, with someone else reacting, moved to the moment everyone's about to be in.
- **Slide 1:** a vintage family Thanksgiving photo, like iluvdealz's, with "pov: you show up to thanksgiving after 3 weeks of this".
- **Slide 2:** a fake iMessage from "Aunt Linda 🦃": "ok who did your skin??" / "be honest is it botox" / you: "it's a $20 serum" / "SEND ME THE LINK" / "your cousin wants one too".
- **Slide 3:** the product on the dinner table with "the only thing I'm sharing at thanksgiving".
- **Products:** RoC lip volumizer, Medicube milk serum, Dr.Melaxin wrap mask.
- **Timing:** post Nov 5 to 25, then turn it into "your mom at Christmas".

### 3. Black Friday cart, before it sells out
Nobody's posting this yet, and it's the biggest sales week of the year.
- **Slide 1:** a screenshot of a TikTok Shop cart with 6 items and the total (e.g. "$61.40") with "my black friday cart 🛒 (adding these now before they sell out)".
- **Slides 2-6:** each item, one per slide, with one line each: "the lip oil everyone's gatekeeping", "the toothpaste with the 40% off", and so on.
- **Last slide:** "screenshot this for black friday 📸".
- **Why it can trend:** a cart is a list of 6 shoppable products in one post. "Screenshot this" drives saves, and saves push the post.
- **Post it twice:** once the week before Black Friday, then again on the day as "it's live, go go go".

### 4. Faceless "she" outfits, but for beauty
This is holistic.wellness.glow's 1.2M format, but with your products. Nobody's applied it to beauty.
- **2 slides, no face, tiny serif text, a cozy bedroom or Christmas-lit vanity.**
  - Slide 1: hands holding the product at a candlelit vanity, "obsessed with this."
  - Slide 2: the product lined up with its set, "for the glass skin girls (sorry about the price)".
- **Why:** it's cheap to make (styled or AI-made photos, no face), and it reads as aesthetic, not an ad. You can post 2-3 a day without it feeling like the same post.

### 5. Ugly Christmas sweater, first
iluvdealz owns Thanksgiving shirts. Christmas sweater season starts after Thanksgiving, and the slot is open.
- **Slide 1:** a vintage family Christmas photo, or an office party photo, with "imagine pulling up to the office party in this".
- **Slide 2:** the sweater flat-laid on a Christmas table, "(sorry about that price)".
- **Why:** it's not girly, guys and moms buy it, and the format is proven at 588K. Be the account that posts it first.
- **Find the sweater:** search Kalodata for funny graphic Christmas sweaters with 15%+ commission. Holiday POD shirts pay well.

### 6. "Help me pick" (the comment bait nobody uses)
None of the 30 winners asks the viewer anything. Comments push slideshows hard.
- **Slide 1:** 4 gift options in a 2x2 grid, numbered, with "which one do I get my mom for christmas?? (be honest)".
- **Slides 2-5:** each option with its price and one line.
- **Last slide:** "comment the number 👇".
- **Why:** every comment counts, and people love voting. The products work for both girls and moms.

### 7. Countdown hooks for the holidays
Your 4th-best post ever was "halloween is in 7 days and you STILL don't have a costume" (5.2K). Do the same for the next three moments:
- "thanksgiving is in 10 days and you STILL don't have an outfit…"
- "it's 3 weeks till christmas and you STILL haven't got her anything…"
- "black friday is in 5 days and your cart is still empty…"

Use the same 3-slide setup as your Halloween post, and the urgency changes daily.

### 8. A "group chat roast" for guys (non-girly lane)
This is iluvdealz's humor, made for guys buying for their boys.
- **Slide 1:** a fake group chat named "the boys 🏈": "who's doing secret santa this year" / "$25 limit" / "if anyone gets me socks again I'm leaving".
- **Slide 2:** a funny or useful gift with "secret santa sorted 🎁".
- **Last slide:** the product with "under $25. you're welcome".
- **Products:** funny graphic tees or sweaters, car gadgets, a novelty mug. Guys' stocking stuffers are wide open on TikTok Shop slides.
