# @brockstaaa: products to post (Kalodata screen)

Oct 25, 2026. Data comes from Kalodata (US TikTok Shop) and cost about 1.5 points.

## How I filtered

- **Category:** Beauty & Personal Care.
- **Sold through affiliates:** yes, with commission of 10% or more.
- **Price:** $8–60, which is impulse territory for a girl audience.
- **Big brand:** the seller is one of the **top 300 beauty shops by 30-day revenue**. That's the stand-in for "has real GMV Max budget". A shop doing six or seven figures a month is buying ads behind its products, so your slides ride that spend. Every product below passes. "Shop #" is the seller's rank.
- **Rising:** revenue growth vs the period before. I used two windows:
  - 7-day growth, with at least $20K revenue this week
  - 30-day growth, with at least $80K revenue this month

  A huge % on a small base is noise. A huge % on $100K+ is a real wave.
- **Comeback:** the product launched 1+ years ago and is growing hard again.
- **Proven giants:** top 7-day revenue, whether or not it's growing. These already have ready buyers, and a good slide just tips them over.
- **Cut for this audience:** men's cologne, trimmers, men's grooming and Halloween items.
