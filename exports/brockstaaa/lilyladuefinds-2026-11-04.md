# @lilyladuefinds: how the $50K account works (pulled Nov 4)

44K followers. Her main account (@lilyladue) is a lifestyle/haul account; this is the shop account.

## The numbers
- **40 posts in 2 days (~20 a day)**, every one a TikTok Shop slideshow, 2 to 5 slides, **no caption**.
- Median about **1,000 views**. Best of the last 40: 13.8K. Worst: 58.
- Her all-time hits: **2M to 8.7M views**, mostly 2-slide posts from Jun to Aug 2026.

## The pattern: few products, lots of hooks
In the posts I looked at, the same product slide (her hand holding it at her bathroom vanity or shelf) is reused across many posts. Only slide 1 (the photo + hook) changes.

## What it means
1. **The angle decides it, not the product.** Same lollipop: the weed-replacement angle got 13.8K, the anxious-flyer angle got 164.
2. **Her winning angles are about a guy, or a taboo:** your ex wants you back, he falls in love, no guy will kiss you, getting ready for him, quitting weed. Same engine as your 46K lips post: what other people will think or do.
3. **The product slide is shot once and reused.** That's how she makes 20 a day.
4. **Most posts flop.** The money comes from volume plus a rare 2M to 8M hit.

## For you
Pick 5 to 8 products. Shoot one clean product slide for each, in the same spot. Then each day write 3 to 4 new slide-1 hooks per product, built on "what he / they will think or do". Kill an angle that's under 500 at 48 hours. Repeat an angle over 3K on the other products.
