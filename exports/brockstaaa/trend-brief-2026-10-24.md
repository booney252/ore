These did worst:

- "if you do NOT want to give up iced coffee…" (whitening strips)
- reposts of the costume once Halloween got close

**Your formula.** Every winner is three slides:
1. **The dream result.** A pretty girl, a mirror selfie, great hair or lips, plus a question her friends ask her.
2. **The before or the secret.** "sleep in this", "1 week ago", the fake text.
3. **The product on your vanity,** with "you're welcome" or "thank me later".

**What wins and what doesn't:**
- **Hooks that sell a result win.** Ones that open on something gross or a fear lose: hair falling out, stained teeth.
- **Hair is your lane.** 4 of your top 6 are hair.
- **Repeating a winner works.** The blowout hook hit twice.

## 3. What this audience buys from November to December (last year's data)

- **Beauty is TikTok Shop's #1 category at Black Friday:** $109M of $500M+ in 2025, up 50%.
- **November is the peak month.** December stays high but drops.
- **Sets win.**
  - Medicube's Glass Glow 8-piece set was the #1 product on all of TikTok Shop in Q4 2025.
  - Medicube is still the top beauty brand this year, including its body care set, PDRN and rosemary oil. You already sell their rosemary oil.
- **Fragrance:** 71% of beauty shoppers planned to buy it. Cheap "smells expensive" dupes for $20–35 sell well, e.g. Bella Vita and QAWAFI.
- **People shop for themselves:** 54% planned to treat themselves.
- **This September's searches:** #giftsforwomen +440%, #holidaygiftideas +248%, #christmaspajamas +297%.
- **TikTok Shop's Black Friday campaign runs Nov 11–30.** Black Friday is Nov 27 and Cyber Monday is Nov 30.
- **Christmas shipping cutoffs:** USPS Ground Advantage Dec 17, Priority Dec 18. Items sold Nov 11–Dec 31 can be returned until Feb 10, which helps "safe to gift" slides.

4. **Lip plumper and holiday lip sets.**
   - Your filler hook worked.
   - Next: a dupe of the Rhode-style holiday lip set, and lip trios as stocking stuffers.
   - Post Dec 1–22.
5. **Perfume dupes for $20–35.** "smell expensive for $20" for gifts and self-gifting. Post Dec 1–20.
6. **Advent calendars,** beauty or hair. Post Nov 15–30, since they have to arrive before Dec 1.
7. **Satin bonnet / silk pillowcase.** Fits your hair lane and works as a cheap stocking stuffer. Post Dec 1–20.
8. **Face tools:** lymphatic drainage, gua sha, fascia rings. Breaking out now. Post Nov 11–30.
9. **Body care:** body glaze, scented body sets. Post Nov 11–Dec 15.
10. **Cozy self-care:** Christmas pajamas, fleece-lined leggings. Searches are rising, but it's off your hair and beauty lane, so test it.

Skip:
- whitening strips and scalp or hair-loss products (they flopped for you)
- anything that opens on a gross or scary problem
- Halloween, from now on

Check before you post: whether each product is on TikTok Shop, its commission and its stock. Rhode itself isn't sold on TikTok Shop, so use a dupe that is.
