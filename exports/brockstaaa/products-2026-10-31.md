# @brockstaaa: products starting to pop (Kalodata, Oct 31)

US TikTok Shop data. It cost about 2.3 Kalodata points (62 left).

## How I screened

- **Categories:** Beauty & Personal Care, plus Health (for the gummies you might copy).
- **Filters:** sold through affiliates, 10%+ commission, $8 to $60.
- **"Starting to pop"** means one of two things:
  - Fast growth this week vs last week, from a small but real base ($3K to $50K a week).
  - Or a launch in the last ~6 weeks that's already selling.
- **Made by videos:** I kept products where most of the sales come from creator videos. A product selling through live streams or its own shop page won't move with slides. "Video %" below is the share of sales that comes from videos.
- **Shop #** is the seller's rank among beauty shops by 30-day sales. A low number means a big brand that's probably paying for ads behind it.
- **Cut:** men's products, Halloween, and anything where videos make under 40% of sales.
