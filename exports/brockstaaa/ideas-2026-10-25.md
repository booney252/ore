## 3. Content ideas to use on any product

These come from the report's patterns and aren't tied to one product:

1. **Someone else reacts.** A fake text from him or a friend on slide 1 or 2, product last. This is the lever behind your best hooks and the biggest shop winners.
2. **Expensive alternative first, then the price.** "$1,200 vs $36", "the filler look without the filler money." Leave the price off now and then so people click the link.
3. **Date deadlines during the sale (Nov 11–30).** The fake Facebook post style: "LADIES don't forget Nov 27–30 the ___ is literally pocket change."
4. **An identity call-out in the hook.** "If you love your girl…", "for the girl who…", "if you're a night sweater…" She either sees herself or sends it to a friend.
5. **Answer "is it junk?" before the close.** "the quality must not be that good right? NOPE." Add a review screenshot or a texture close-up.
6. **Sets and counts.** "All 7 pieces", "5 pack." A number reads as value at a glance.
7. **Organic lists with one tagged product.** "everything shower routine", "it girl christmas wishlist", "things your girlfriend wants but won't ask for", with your product as one of the items. These lists get saved and shared.
8. **Post winners again with small changes.** The shop accounts repost the same slideshow 2–3 times with new photos or wording. Your blowout hook already won twice. When a post hits 3x your usual, make 2 more versions.
