Build a brand people buy from.

People follow a person and buy from a person. We studied 9 creators who sell a course, coaching, a service or a community, to see how they grow without posting ads all day. Here is what they do, and how to copy it.

Based on about 680 Instagram reels from Jianna Capri, Shelby Sapp, Ellie Snow, Tommy Cetty, Jordan Welch, Ray, Luke Alexander, Noah Frydberg and Nico, pulled in October 2026. "2x" means twice that creator's usual views.

The big idea

Most of what they post isn't selling.

Across all 9, direct pitches are only about 5% to 40% of posts. The rest earns attention and trust, so the few asks actually work.

The posts that got the most reach were life moments, opinions and call-outs, usually with no call to action at all. The pitch rode on teaching and proof posts, and it almost always gave away something free.

What they all do

Eight habits that show up across the accounts.

Habit 1

One identity line, everywhere

Viewers learn who you are in a single sentence, repeated until it sticks.

7 years ago I was a barista making $8/hr

Shelby, in 44 captions. Jianna opens every video with her 90-day challenge. Ellie keeps saying "at 21".

Habit 2

Reach posts don't sell

Life and opinion posts got the most views and almost never had a call to action. Shelby's 27 wedding posts had zero pitches and beat her usual by 1.9x.

Habit 3

The ask rides on value

The comment keyword sits on teaching and proof posts. Jianna's tool demos (9 of 13 have a CTA) lost no reach for it.

Habit 4

Give something free

The CTA offers a guide, a list, a class or a DM. The paid offer is rarely named. Jianna never names one. Shelby names her academy about 4 times in 93 posts.

Habit 5

A number or a real name in the hook

How I run 400 AI influencers at once

Noah's top 5 all lead with a big specific number. Tommy's top 4 all name a real account or a real price.

Habit 6

A story with stakes, told as a series

Jianna's "$100K in 90 days", Ellie's "$100K a month at 21", Shelby's wedding countdown. People come back to see what happens next.

Habit 7

Pick a villain

Fake gurus, scams, "larpers", bad advice. Calling something out beat a neutral tip for Tommy, Luke, Jianna and Nico.

Habit 8

Post a lot, rerun winners

Noah and Nico post about 3 a day and rerun scripts that hit. Shelby posts about 2 a day. Volume gives the winners a chance to show up.

Build yours

Five steps to set up your pillars this week.

1

Write your identity line

Where you started, where you are, in one sentence. "Six months ago I was [before]. Now I [after]." Use it in your bio, your captions and your intros.

2

Pick your villain

What do you stand against? Fake gurus, a bad method, an old way of working. That's your Stand pillar.

3

Start a story with stakes

A goal with a number and a deadline, posted as a series. "$10K by New Year's, day 1." That's your Proof pillar, and it gives people a reason to come back.

4

List 10 things you can teach

Methods, tools, mistakes, systems. Each one is a Teach post, and each can end on a keyword for something free.

5

Make one free thing to give away

A guide, a checklist, a short class. Every Ask post offers it. Sell the paid offer in the DMs and on calls, not in the feed.
