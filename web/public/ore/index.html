The problem

The Legacy Ad Trap.

Everyone finds "winners" by sorting an ad library by longest running. Those are mostly ads brands forgot to turn off, so teams copy what worked months ago. And when they do find a real winner, they copy its surface, the format and the wording, without knowing which parts carry it.

How it works

From "what's working?" to briefs in your creators' hands.

Step 1

Catch the Breakout Signal

Add your competitors once. Ore reads each one's top 10 ads every day and flags any new ad that breaks in.

Step 2

Find the load-bearing elements

Every breakout gets transcribed and taken apart: the hook, the first 3 seconds, the structure, the proof, the offer. Ore marks which parts carry the ad, so you keep the 10% that made it work.

Step 3

Prove

Each idea comes with its receipts: days live, versions, other brands on the same angle, and quotes from real customers with links.

Step 4

Brief

You get ready-to-film scripts for your product, written for your creators, with the "why we're making this" your team will ask for.

Questions

What people ask first.

Does AI write my ads?

Ore does the research and drafts the briefs. You choose the ideas and edit the words, and every edit teaches it your brand. The judgment stays with you.

How is this different from an ad library or a swipe file?

Those show you what's running. Ore watches each competitor's top 10 every day, flags the new ads they just put money behind, explains which parts make them work, and turns that into briefs for your product.

Which platforms does it cover?

Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram), plus organic Reels, TikToks and YouTube Shorts from the creator accounts you want to learn from.

Do I need to connect my ad account?

No. Ore works from public ad libraries and public posts.

Will my competitors see my briefs?

No. Your research, notes and briefs are private to your brand, and your briefs are written from your own brand memory.
