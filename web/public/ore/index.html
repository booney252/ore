Questions

What people ask first.

Does AI write the scripts?

Ore researches what's winning and writes the script. You review it once, edit anything, and every edit teaches it your brand. Then it writes each creator's version in their voice.

Will anything go to my creators without me?

No. Nothing goes to a creator until you approve it, and you can edit any line first.

How does Ore decide what's winning?

Every competitor ad gets a grade from A to F with the reason in plain words: how long it's been running, whether it's still live, how many versions they've made, and whether it just broke into their top 10.

Which platforms does it cover?

Meta ads from competing apps, including the ones they run through creators' pages, their App Store reviews for the words your users actually use, plus Reels, TikToks and YouTube Shorts from creator accounts.

How does Ore know which videos worked?

Every script has a short code. Upload your ads export from Meta and Ore matches each ad to its script and creator: spend, cost per install or trial, hook rate. A direct Meta connection is coming.

Does it work if we're not an app?

Yes. Ore works for any brand with a creator program. We started with apps because that's where we've proven it.

The teardown is free. Plans start at $129 a month.
