Questions

What people ask first.

Does AI write the scripts?

Ore does the research and drafts each brief. You choose and edit, and every edit teaches it your brand and your creators. Creators still say it in their own words.

Do my creators need an account?

No. You send each creator a link to their brief.

How does Ore know who my creators are?

You add a short profile for each one: who they are, their story with your product, what they film, how they talk and what they can honestly claim. Ore uses it in every brief for them.

Does it handle payments, contracts or finding creators?

No. Keep the tools you use for that. Ore covers what each creator should make and which of their videos work.

Which platforms does it cover?

Meta ads from your competitors, plus Reels, TikToks and YouTube Shorts from creator accounts.

Will my competitors see my briefs?

No. Your research, roster and briefs are private to your brand.
